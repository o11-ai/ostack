Write each first message for one person, from their research notes, in the user's voice (`voice.md`). If the user gave a template, follow it, filling every part from real research.

Default shape when there is no template:
1. **Opening:** one or two sentences built on an observed fact, tied to a task or decision the reader has.
2. **Offer:** one sentence from `about.md`, in the reader's terms: the outcome, not the internals.
3. **Ask:** exactly one, the ask from `about.md`.
4. **Signature:** exactly as the user gave it.

Emails get a short, specific subject a colleague might write. Never fake `re:` or `fwd:`. LinkedIn notes and messages are one or two lines with no signature.

Before reserving, check:
- Every fact comes from a source you read. Unknown pain is asked about, never asserted.
- Swap test: if another company's name would work just as well, it is too generic. Research more or defer the lead.
- No unfilled placeholders (`reserve` rejects them too).
- Vary openings across a batch; never rotate one template with noun swaps.

For previews and examples, show the company and URL, two sentences on what they do, why they qualify (or what is unconfirmed), then the full message. Previews never reserve or send.
