# Offer

## The company
One sentence: what you do, and for whom.

## Traction
Real numbers only: revenue, growth, customers, retention. Include dates.

## The round
Size, stage, and what has already been committed (if shareable).

## The ask
For example: "open to 20 minutes next week?"

## Never claim
Unconfirmed commitments, projections presented as results, or investor names without permission.
